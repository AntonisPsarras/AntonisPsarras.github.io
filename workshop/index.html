          SYS_00 // CONTROLS
          
        
          Getting around
          
        
          
          
          
            
          // GUIDED
          
            
            
              
          • Choose a location from the list, or use ‹ ›.
            • 
              
          • Click or tap an object (or its button in the list) to inspect it.
            • 
              
          • Drag to look around a little.
            • 
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
          // EXPLORE
          
            
            
              
          • Move the mouse to look, like a first-person game. Esc frees the cursor; click the view to take it back.
            • 
              
          • WASD or ↑↓ to walk, Shift to run, ←→ to turn.
            • 
              
          • E, F, Enter or a click interacts with whatever is under the dot. The label next to the dot says what.
            • 
              
          • Phone or tablet: a walking pad and an Interact button appear at the bottom. Drag anywhere else to look, or tap an object. The Pad chip at the top turns them on or off.
            • 
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
          // SHORTCUTS
          
            
            
              
          • G guided · X explore · L lights · M sound
            • 
              
          • Esc closes or steps back · ? this panel · ` diagnostics
            •
